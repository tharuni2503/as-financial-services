Eligibility guidance

These are general indicators, not a pre-approval. Your lender makes the final assessment after reviewing your complete application.

Applicant age
Typically 21–65 years

Varies by lender and co-applicant profile.

Salaried income
Stable monthly income with proof

Self-employed income

Contribution
Margin money as per lender

Loan-to-value limits depend on loan amount and property value.

No eligibility guarantee. The information above is general guidance only. Confirm the current criteria with the lender before making a property commitment.

Documentation

A checklist that keeps the file moving

Exact requirements vary by lender and applicant profile. This is the common core of a home loan application.

  • PAN and Aadhaar (identity and address proof)
  • Latest salary slips and bank statements, or ITR and business financials
  • Employment or business proof
  • Property documents, chain of title and approved plan
  • Existing loan statements, where applicable
  • Photographs and signed application forms

Never share original documents without a verified process and a written acknowledgement. Confirm the lender’s official collection method before handing anything over.

A loan against property, clearly explained

A loan against property uses residential or commercial property as security. The amount depends on the property’s assessed value, your income and the lender’s loan-to-value policy. Because the property is at risk, it deserves a careful conversation.

  • Residential and commercial property

  • Clear explanation of charges
