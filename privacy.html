Privacy Policy

How this demonstration website handles information — and what still needs to be confirmed before it goes live.

Last updated 07 November 2026

1. Who we are

AS Financial Services and Solutions is a loan advisory service based in Hyderabad, Telangana, India. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to AS Financial Services and Solutions. We are not a bank, NBFC or lender.

The contact details published on this site — phone, WhatsApp, email and address — are verified. Opening hours were not provided, so please confirm availability before visiting.

2. Information this website collects

The enquiry form asks for your name, mobile number, email address, loan type, optional loan amount and an optional message. In this build, that information is processed only inside your browser to validate the form. It is not transmitted to a server, database, email inbox or third party, and it is not stored by us.

If you close or refresh the page, the form contents are lost. The validation summary can be copied to your clipboard at your request; that action is handled by your browser and is not recorded by us.

3. Cookies, analytics and local storage

This build does not set advertising cookies, run analytics or use local storage to profile visitors. Fonts and JavaScript libraries are hosted on this website rather than loaded from third-party font or script networks.

The contact page embeds a Google Maps iframe. Loading that map may allow Google to receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details, subject to Google’s own privacy terms. If you prefer not to load the map, use the direct Google Maps link or avoid the embedded map.

4. How information would be used

If a backend is connected in a future version, enquiry details would be used to:

  • Respond to your question or callback request.
  • Understand the loan type and property requirement you have described.
  • Share relevant information about lenders, documentation or next steps.
  • Keep a record of the conversation for service quality and legal compliance.

We would not sell your personal information. Any sharing with a lender would happen only where it is necessary to progress an enquiry you have asked us to support, or where the law requires it.

5. Retention and security

Because no enquiry data is transmitted or stored in this build, there is no retention period to describe yet. A production version should define how long enquiries are kept, who can access them and how they are protected. Access should be limited to people who need it, and sensitive documents should be collected through verified, secure channels rather than ordinary email or messaging.

6. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its rules as implemented, you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal data, withdraw consent, or raise a grievance. Because this build does not store enquiry data, there may be nothing for us to retrieve. Once a production backend is connected, this section should explain how to exercise those rights and who to contact.

7. Children

This website is intended for adults considering financial services. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Third-party links

This website links to Google Maps and may link to lender websites. Those services have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their content or data practices.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website develops, particularly when a production enquiry system, analytics or additional third-party services are introduced. The updated date at the top of this page will change when that happens.

10. Contact

For questions about this policy, use the enquiry form on the contact page or call, WhatsApp or email us using the verified details published on this site. Opening hours were not provided, so please confirm availability before visiting.
