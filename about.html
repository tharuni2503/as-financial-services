About AS Financial

Our approach

Finance should feel simpler than the paperwork

Whether you are buying a home, funding a business, planning education or reviewing insurance, the decisions around money can feel heavy. AS Financial Services and Solutions exists to help you understand your options before you commit, and to keep the process moving afterwards.

We work with you to compare structures, prepare a consistent file and anticipate the questions a lender or provider is likely to ask. Where a product may not suit your situation, we will say so plainly.

We are an advisory service, not a bank or NBFC. Every loan is assessed solely by the lender and remains subject to its policy, eligibility criteria and documentation requirements.

How we work

Every conversation starts with your requirement, your timeline and the property or purpose you are considering. From there we can discuss the shape of the finance, the documents likely to be needed and the questions worth asking a lender or provider.

Before you apply

  • Understand how loan-to-value affects your down payment.
  • Compare the full cost of borrowing, not only the headline rate.
  • Check whether a floating or fixed structure better fits your plans.
  • Prepare a consistent set of income and property documents.

While the file is moving

We help you respond to lender requests, keep track of what has been submitted and understand the next milestone. The lender remains responsible for verification, credit assessment and the final sanction decision.

What we are not

We are not a bank, NBFC or lender. We do not approve loans, set interest rates or guarantee timelines. Any rate or fee shown on this website is an indicative sample for demonstration and must be confirmed with the lender.
