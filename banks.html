How to read the numbers

Four things that change the real cost

A comparison is only useful if it reflects the whole loan. Use these checks before treating any rate as the best option.

The benchmark

Floating rates move with the lender’s benchmark. Ask how and when resets happen.

The fees

Processing, legal, technical and valuation charges can change the effective cost.

The tenure

A longer tenure lowers the EMI but usually increases total interest paid.

The eligibility

The best advertised rate is irrelevant if your profile does not qualify for it.
